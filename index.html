Is the ground dry?
Pilot area, NASA soil moisture
Red line is the selected season, grey lines are the other seasons, dashed is the 5 season average. Root zone soil wetness (0 is bone dry, 1 is saturated) from NASA POWER MERRA-2, the grid cell nearest the pilot farms (about 50 km wide, so it shows the regional trend, not one field). Hot days are days with a max of 32°C or more in that cell. Only 5 seasons are loaded, so the average is a 5 season average. This is context for the alerts and does not change their score yet.


Do the alerts match real harvests?
Pilot point, 30 seasons
Each dot is one season from 1995/96 to 2024/25. Seasons with more alert weeks had lower soy yields across Argentina. 
Alert weeks count weeks with a 4 week rain deficit over 30%, or 3 or more hot days at the growth stage. Yield is Argentina national soy yield compared with its long term trend. This stands in for loan defaults, which we do not have, and one weather point stands in for the whole country.

Crop value assumptions

Crop value under normal climate is hectares times yield times price. Edit any yield or price and every value above updates. Repayment model: production costs are 50% of normal crop value, each loan owes its own principal plus interest, and a stress score of 100 means a 60% yield loss.




Sources: Argentina wheat, corn and soy output and area · Bolivia soy yield bulletin · Colombia rice price and yield. Prices marked placeholder are rough starting points, not market quotes.
