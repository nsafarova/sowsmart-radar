Crop value assumptions

Crop value under normal climate is hectares times yield times price. Edit any yield or price and every value above updates. Repayment model: production costs are 50% of normal crop value, each loan owes its own principal plus interest, and a stress score of 100 means a 60% yield loss.

Sources: Argentina wheat, corn and soy output and area · Bolivia soy yield bulletin · Colombia rice price and yield. Prices marked placeholder are rough starting points, not market quotes.
